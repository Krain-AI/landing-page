### **Introducing Krain AI**

Krain is building the infrastructure layer for the AI economy—combining discovery, deployment, liquidity, and trust into a unified platform. As AI adoption accelerates, developers and users are held back by scattered tools, opaque token launches, and fragmented deployment options. Krain solves this by offering a vertically integrated system that makes it easy to launch, scale, and grow AI applications.

At the heart of the Krain ecosystem are three core components:

**AI App Portal** A structured, searchable directory that simplifies discovery across the AI landscape. Users can browse, compare, and evaluate AI apps and agents by use case, performance, and reviews—enhancing visibility, trust, and adoption.

**Token Launch System** Launching a tokenized AI app should be simple, fair, and strategically aligned with long-term success. Krain’s Token Launch System offers three distinct paths to suit different teams, communities, and goals—each supported by Krain’s infrastructure, distribution, and liquidity architecture.

**Launch Types**

- **Social Launch** A community-first token launch using a bonding curve model. This approach lets early users buy in at lower prices while allowing supply and demand to naturally determine valuation. It’s ideal for grassroots growth, community-led projects, and viral distribution.

- **FastPass Launch** Skip the bonding curve and launch directly into a **Guaranteed Liquidity Pool (GLP)** backed by the KRAIN ecosystem. FastPass projects receive promotional support across Krain’s platform and access to streamlined launch tooling. It’s designed for serious teams ready to scale fast.

- **VIP Launch** A white-glove, fully supported launch experience. Krain’s team provides hands-on advisory services, technical setup, tokenomics review, and go-to-market strategy. This option is for top-tier projects looking for maximum polish and reach from day one.

**Liquidity Architecture**

- **Guaranteed Liquidity Pools (GLP)** GLPs lock liquidity for five years to ensure deep, reliable markets. They generate protocol fees that flow back to the KRAIN treasury, creating a sustainable revenue engine aligned with platform growth.

- **Infinite Liquidity Pools (ILP)** ILPs are designed for long-term yield, giving **KDEX token holders** exposure to trading activity and upside across the ecosystem. This structure rewards ecosystem participation while reducing liquidity fragmentation across newly launched tokens.

**AI App Hosting** A seamless deployment layer that lets developers launch and monetize their AI apps without infrastructure headaches. With built-in monitoring, monetization tools, and plug-and-play integrations with the App Portal and Token Launch System, hosting on Krain drastically reduces time-to-market.

Krain exists to unlock the full potential of decentralized AI—empowering developers, rewarding early users, and giving AI app users meaningful exposure to the apps shaping the future.

## Token Launch System

Launching a token is one of the most powerful ways for AI app developers to bootstrap users, raise capital, and align incentives. But traditional launchpads often fall short—offering little more than temporary hype, fragile liquidity, and minimal post-launch support.

Krain’s Token Launch System is designed from the ground up to serve the unique needs of AI-native ecosystems. It provides a flexible, infrastructure-backed framework for launching tokens that scale with your growth, foster real utility, and generate long-term momentum.

##### Key Features

**Multiple Launch Paths**

Krain offers three tailored launch options to fit different project stages and goals:

- **Social Launch** A bonding curve–based model that rewards early community participation and enables organic price discovery. Ideal for community-first projects looking to build strong grassroots traction.

- **FastPass Launch** Designed for high-readiness teams. FastPass lets you bypass the bonding curve and launch directly with a **Guaranteed Liquidity Pool (GLP)**, while receiving featured promotion across Krain’s ecosystem.

- **VIP Launch** A white-glove experience that includes tokenomics advisory, go-to-market planning, and end-to-end launch support. Best suited for high-potential teams seeking a polished, strategic debut.

**Guaranteed Liquidity Pools (GLP)**

Every FastPass and VIP launch includes a GLP—liquidity locked for five years to ensure deep trading pools and price stability from day one. These pools also generate platform fees that flow back to the KRAIN treasury, creating a self-sustaining launch ecosystem.

**Infinite Liquidity Pools (ILP)**

ILPs provide decentralized, scalable liquidity infrastructure that evolves with ecosystem growth. They ensure seamless trading for new tokens, reduce fragmentation, and enhance long-term network reliability.

**Built-in Distribution**

Launched tokens are integrated directly into the AI App Portal and eligible for ecosystem-wide visibility, helping you turn launch momentum into lasting adoption.

##### Why It Matters

Most token launchpads are built for speculation. Krain’s Token Launch System is built for builders. By combining trusted liquidity structures, flexible launch formats, and deep post-launch integration, we give teams the tools to launch with confidence and scale with purpose.

Whether you’re looking to rally a community, go to market fast, or launch with expert support, Krain’s Token Launch System adapts to your vision and helps you build a durable foundation for your AI-native economy.
